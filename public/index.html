Privacy policy

Last updated 28 September 2026

Who we are

Student Friendly is a free pilot project run by [YOUR FULL NAME] (“we”). We are the controller of your personal data under UK GDPR. Contact: [YOUR CONTACT EMAIL].

What we collect

  • Account details: name, phone number, email address, course and the job field you are interested in.
  • Resume and LinkedIn details you choose to type in: education, experience, projects, skills, awards and similar.
  • CV text you paste for a CV check. We do not store the text itself, only its word count and the type of suggestion given.
  • Your checklist ticks and search words (used to fetch results, not stored against your profile).
  • Technical data: a login session (30 days, stored hashed) and your IP address in short-term server logs, used for security and to limit abuse.

Why we use it, and our legal basis

  • To create your account, log you in, suggest vacancies and write your resume and LinkedIn content: your consent. You can withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent abuse: legitimate interests.

We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or pass it to employers or recruiters. We do not make automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you. AI-written text can contain mistakes, so always check it before you use it.

Who else handles your data

  • AI provider (Anthropic or OpenAI, depending on how the service is set up): receives your course, job field, search words and the resume or CV details you submit, so it can write suggestions. We do not send your name, phone number or email address. Anything you type in free text (for example an employer’s name) is included.
  • Adzuna (vacancy data): receives the keywords and location you search for.
  • Google (Sign in with Google): confirms who you are. We receive your name and verified email address from Google; we never see your Google password.
  • Hosting provider (for example Render): stores the website and database.

Some of these providers may process data outside the UK. Where they do, we rely on recognised safeguards such as the UK’s adequacy regulations, the International Data Transfer Agreement or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Until you delete your account. Deleting it (Account → Delete my account) permanently removes your profile, resume details, checklist and history. Login sessions expire after 30 days. Hosting logs are kept briefly by our hosting provider.

Your rights

You can access your data (Account → Download my data), correct it (edit your profile), delete it, restrict or object to our use of it, receive it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. To use a right we cannot offer in the app, email us. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies and local storage

We do not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. Your browser stores one login token in local storage so you stay logged in; it is removed when you log out.

Security

Session tokens are stored hashed, traffic is sent over HTTPS and requests are rate limited. No system is perfectly secure, so please tell us straight away if you notice a problem.

Age

Student Friendly is intended for people aged 16 or over.

Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell registered users by email.
